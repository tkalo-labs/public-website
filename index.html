Why Tkalo

Many threads. One coherent whole.

Tkalo is rooted in the South Slavic idea of weaving. The name reflects how we work: software, data, models, evidence, research, and domain knowledge are separate threads. The useful result comes from structuring them into something coherent, explainable, and durable.

Principles

Research-minded. Engineering-grounded.

Start from the problem Tools follow the domain. Architecture follows the constraints.
Make behavior explicit Systems should be inspectable, testable, and understandable—not merely impressive.
Measure what matters Ideas earn their place through evidence, benchmarks, and real-world outcomes.
